# Balkan Smart DJ — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026

Balkan Smart DJ ("Bot," "Service," "we," "us," or "our") is a Discord music bot providing music playback, personalized recommendations, and optional user-specific music preference learning.

We respect user privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate these features.

### 1. Information We Process

**1.1. Basic Discord Information**

When you interact with the Bot, it may process:

- Your Discord User ID.
- Discord server and channel identifiers.
- Slash commands and their parameters.
- Voice channel membership information required for playback.
- Information needed to manage music queues.

This information is used to operate the requested features.

**1.2. Optional Music Preference Information**

If you enable personal learning using `/learning enable`, the Bot stores information associated with your Discord User ID, including:

- Requested songs and their associated artists and titles.
- Song likes and dislikes.
- Explicit song skips.
- Track identifiers, categories, and genres.
- Timestamps of recorded interactions.
- Whether personal learning is enabled.

This information is stored to develop an individual music preference profile.

Personal learning is disabled by default.

**1.3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect**

The current Bot does not require or intentionally store:

- Discord passwords or authentication credentials belonging to users.
- Spotify account passwords.
- Private Discord messages or ordinary chat message contents.
- Voice recordings or microphone audio.
- Payment card information.
- Precise geographic location.

### 2. How We Use Information

We process information for the following purposes:

- Executing music commands and managing playback.
- Maintaining music queues.
- Remembering opted-in users' music preferences.
- Producing personalized song recommendations.
- Improving recommendation relevance from likes, dislikes, and requests.
- Identifying technical errors and maintaining service functionality.
- Preventing misuse and protecting the Service.

Music recommendations are generated automatically using interaction history and recommendation algorithms.

These recommendations do not make legally significant decisions about users.

### 3. Personalized Recommendations

Each opted-in user receives a separate music preference profile linked to their Discord User ID.

The profile can be used across Discord servers where the same Bot installation and database operate.

The recommendation system may compare opted-in users' musical preferences to identify potentially relevant songs.

Individual listening histories are not intentionally published to other users. However, song requests, queues, and playback announcements may be visible to members of the relevant Discord server.

The Bot does not require a paid external AI provider to perform its preference-learning functions.

### 4. Consent and Controls

Music preference learning is optional.

Available privacy controls include:

- `/learning enable` — enable storage of personal music preferences.
- `/learning disable` — stop recording new preference interactions while retaining previously stored preferences.
- `/learning forget` — delete your stored user profile, request history, and ratings from the active learning database.
- `/mytaste` — view a summary of your personal music preferences.

Disabling learning is not the same as deleting previously stored data.

Users may contact the operator to request assistance with data access, correction, or deletion.

### 5. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot stores preference information in a database associated with the Bot's hosting environment.

The current implementation uses SQLite.

Personal preference records are retained until deleted by the user or operator, or until the Service is discontinued and the data is no longer required. The current implementation does not include an automatic expiration schedule.

Using `/learning forget` deletes the associated active user record, preference events, and ratings.

Technical logs and any backups maintained by the hosting operator may have separate retention periods. Contact the operator for information about such records.

### 6. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with third-party music platforms or content sources.

**Discord:** Provides user interactions, identity identifiers, server information, and voice channel functionality.

**Online Music Sources:** When online lookup is enabled, music search queries and requests for track information may be transmitted to external services, including YouTube. Those services may process requests under their own privacy policies and terms.

**Hosting Providers:** Infrastructure providers may process information necessary to operate and maintain the Bot.

We do not sell user music preference profiles or use them for targeted advertising.

The current Bot does not connect directly to Spotify accounts or collect Spotify authentication tokens.

### 7. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable privacy law, we rely on an appropriate legal basis for each processing activity.

Processing necessary to respond to user commands supports the requested functionality.

Personalized music preference learning is based on the user's optional, affirmative choice to enable that feature.

Certain limited operational processing may be necessary for service security and technical maintenance, subject to applicable law.

Users may withdraw their participation in personal learning and request deletion of their information.

### 8. International Data Processing

Discord, hosting infrastructure, and external music providers may operate in different countries.

Information may therefore be processed outside a user's country of residence.

Where legally required, the operator will use appropriate protections for international personal data transfers.
